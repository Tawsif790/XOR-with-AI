How it works

  1. The player selects an empty cell.
  2. JavaScript updates the board in memory.
  3. The Minimax algorithm evaluates possible future moves.
  4. The AI selects the best available move.
  5. JavaScript updates the board and checks whether the game has ended.

There is no server and no database. Everything runs in your browser.

The Minimax AI

Minimax looks at every possible future game. The AI (O) picks moves that maximize its score, and assumes you (X) pick moves that minimize it. An AI win scores 10 − depth, a human win depth − 10, and a draw 0. Depth makes the AI win faster and lose slower.
